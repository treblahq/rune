# Brand assets

The canonical Rune source is the approved `rune.svg` supplied with the project.
The frontend keeps transparent monochrome copies under `public/brand/`.

- Light mark fill: `#213130`
- Dark mark fill: `#F7F8F5`
- Keep internal geometry one color.
- Preserve aspect ratio and clear space.
- Do not add gradients, strokes, shadows, or independent internal colors.
- The favicon uses the same geometry on a transparent canvas.
